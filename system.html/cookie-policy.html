Cookie & Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Cookies we use

WasteLess uses one cookie, and only to keep you logged in:

NamePurposeHow long it lasts
sid Remembers that you are logged in (vendor or admin), so you don't have to log in on every page. Until you log out, or 8 hours after logging in, whichever comes first.
  • It is a strictly necessary cookie: the vendor and admin pages cannot work without it, so it is not optional.
  • It holds only a random code, not your name, email or password.
  • Page scripts cannot read it, and it is never sent along when another website links to us.
  • We do not use advertising, tracking or analytics cookies, and we do not share cookies with anyone.

What information we keep

  • Stall accounts: business name, email, contact person and contact number, so the QCU Cooperative admin can manage stalls.
  • Passwords are stored scrambled (hashed). Nobody, including the admin, can see your actual password.
  • Products, stock, sales and feedback you enter, used for your stall's dashboard, reports and the plastic packaging summary.
  • The public landing page only shows totals for all stalls combined, never a single stall's data.

Your choices

  • Log out to remove the cookie right away.
  • You can also delete cookies in your browser settings. You will just need to log in again.
  • To change or remove your stall's information, send a "Stall Info Change" message from the vendor page's Feedback & Reports, or contact the QCU Cooperative admin.
